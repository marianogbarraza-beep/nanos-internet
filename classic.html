About me

there's a person in this machine

I'm Nano Garcia Barraza, a visual designer based in Buenos Aires. Currently Senior Visual Designer at DEPT®, designing for Google.

Images, typography, visual identities. And an unreasonable number of open tabs.

Outside the work folder: photography, technology, games and the weird corners of the internet. I grew up on dial-up, msn, winamp skins and geocities pages with "under construction" gifs. That internet was ugly, slow and completely alive. I try to keep the alive part.

2004: FrontPage, 56k, one GIF per hour. 2026: an idea becomes something you can click in one afternoon. ¿y si armamos lo que sea? The tools got fast. Taste is still the slow part — and the part that matters. → making_of.txt
